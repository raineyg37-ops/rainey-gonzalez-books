Guide to the four paperback editions

Which Me, Right Now! time capsule fits this stage?

The best choice is the edition whose prompts feel comfortable for the person filling it in today. These four books cover different ages and school years. Each link below shows a real page from that exact paperback, so you can see the format before choosing.

Ages 2–4

Simple coloring and “tell me about you” prompts capture little favorites. A grown-up can help record answers when a child is not writing yet. The 24-page paperback has ten activities; the sample asks about a favorite song.

See the ages 2–4 sample and book details →

Pre-K through 4th grade

Ten draw, color and fill-in activities make a snapshot of a child's school-year favorites and ideas. The 24-page paperback includes this sample page for a favorite food, game, story and activity.

See the Pre-K–4th sample and book details →

Grades 5–8

This 32-page paperback has fourteen activities for writing, sketching and recording interests, friendships and goals. The sample invites students to draw and describe their current style.

See the grades 5–8 sample and book details →

High school · grades 9–12

Sixteen activities in a 36-page paperback capture preferences, memories and thoughts about what comes next. The sample is a “This or That?” page with choices to circle and room to make it personal.

See the high-school sample and book details →
Between stages? Look at the sample prompts with the child or teen, then choose the edition they would actually enjoy completing. The grade labels describe the books' intended audiences; there is no test or required reading level.

These are selected page previews, not free printable activity sheets. Each book page links to its exact paperback on Amazon, where current price and availability appear. This is Rainey Gonzalez's own book guide; the artwork was made with AI assistance.
